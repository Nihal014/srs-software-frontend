The value of finished products you have made but not sold yet (for example, samosas and cutlets left on the shelf). Count your stock, work out what it cost to make, and record that amount here.
Each entry is the total value of all unsold stock on that date. Only the newest entry is used, and it is added to your income in Profit & Loss and to the company total. Whenever your stock changes (some sold, new production added), record a fresh entry with the current total. If everything is sold, record 0.
